# Battles 110–112 verification (independent, Opus)

Verifier: independent Opus pass, 2026-11-17. Files checked at commit 4933249 (branch smaps-b): `design/data/110.json`,
`111.json`, `112.json` and `notes-110-112.md`. No data file was edited.

**Result: terrain, units, leader positions and counts match the maps and the survey in all three battles. The errors
are leader names: 3 names that differ from the map labels (110 8,6 and 0,10; 111 0,4) and 1 spelling (110 0,6). There is
also 1 note gap (110, where the 5-block SP/LN stands). 112 matches.**

## Method

- **Maps.** `cnc-napoleonics-research/maps/110.jpg`, `111.jpg`, `112.jpg` (1190×866). Images from this check are in
  `map-work/verify-110-112/`: overlays `ov-NNN.png/`, 3× crops `c110/` `c111/` `c112/`, renders `render-NNN/`, leader
  label zooms `lbl-*.png`, block zooms `z*.png`.
- **Grid.** Each data file's `grid` equals the map's fit in `scripts/maps/grids.json` (110 and 111: 74.38, 129.88; 112:
  77.25, 135.88; w 87.0, pitch 75.5). On the labelled overlays every hex outline sits on the map's hex lines, 112
  included.
- **Independent reading first.** Each map was read from its labelled overlay before the data file was opened for
  comparison: every block (side from frame colour, code from the label and icon), each leader block, and every terrain
  hex. 3× crops (`crops.py`) then covered every unit cluster, hill block, forest, the 110 stream, the towns and the lone
  leaders. 6–7× zooms were used for doubtful blocks.
- **Leader labels.** Each name strip was cropped, rotated upright and enlarged 10–24× (bicubic, contrast and unsharp
  variants). The JPEG text is about 7 px high, so readings rest on glyph count, ascenders and word length. Each was
  then checked against the scenario page's historical notes (WebFetch). The pages name only the commanders and a few
  generals, so the Wikipedia orders of battle for Tamames and Ocaña were checked too.
- **Renders** (`render.py`) of each data file over its map, compared at full size: every tag sits on its block or
  terrain tile.
- **Tools.** `check.py`: ok on all three. `scan.py`: 110 has two flags (3,2 hill 0.10, 3,3 hill 0.11); 111 and 112 have
  none. Both 110 flags were looked at in crops (see below).
- **Counts** were made by hand from the map, not from the file. They agree with the file and with survey 2.1.

### Discrepancies

- **D1. 110 8,6 bottom leader: the data says "Lamartiniere"; the map label reads "Labassee" (Labassée).**
  - The label has 8 glyphs, `L a b a s s e e`, and the third glyph has a tall ascender (`b`).
  - It is shorter than "Marcognet" (9 glyphs) on the 7,1 block. "Lamartiniere" (12 glyphs, short third letter `m`)
    cannot fit.
  - Neither the page nor Wikipedia names this brigadier, so the reading rests on the label. It is clear at 10–24×
    (`lbl-110-bot2.png`, `lbl-110-z.png`).
- **D2. 110 0,10 top leader (on SP/HC): the data says "Anguera"; the map label reads "Anglona".**
  - The glyphs are `A n g l o n a`: the 4th has an ascender, and the word ends `-ona`, not `-era`.
  - The Prince of Anglona commanded the Spanish Cavalry Division at Tamames (Wikipedia order of battle), and this
    leader sits on the Spanish heavy cavalry. "Anguera" matches no general of the battle.
- **D3. 110 0,6 top leader: the data says "Belvedere"; the map label reads "Belveder" (8 glyphs, ending `-der`).**
  - The man is the Conde de Belveder (Wikipedia spells it "Belvedere"), so the identity is right.
  - Only the spelling differs from the label. This is minor and moderately sure: the last glyph reads as `r`, with no
    `e` after it.
- **D4. Notes: the 5-block SP/LN is not located.**
  - Survey 2.5 says non-standard block counts "must be transcribed per unit". The 110 note only calls the 5-block
    SP/LN "a drawing detail".
  - That unit is **SP/LN 3,4** (strength disc "5"; every other SP/LN shows 4).
  - The notes should name the hex, as `105.json` does for its non-standard blocks.

### Remark (not counted)

- **3,1 SP/LN has a striped right face.** Four black diagonal bars run down the side of the block (`z110-ln-blocks.png`).
  No other block on 110–112 has them. Its disc shows 4 and its code is LN, so the key is right. The survey does not
  mention the marking. It may be worth an `art` note or a question to the controller.

### Discrepancy

- **D5. 111 0,4 top leader: the data says "Freire?"; the map label reads "Rivas".**
  - The label has 5 glyphs: a capital with a bowl, a dotted `i`, then `v a s`.
  - It is the same length as "Paris" (5 glyphs) and shorter than "Bernuy". "Freire" would show two `e`s and a
    flat-topped `F`.
  - J. Rivas commanded the 2nd Spanish cavalry division at Ocaña (Wikipedia order of battle). The scenario page names
    only Freire, Milhaud and Paris.
  - Confidence is moderate, so record it as "Rivas" (or "Rivas?") rather than "Freire?". On 112, by contrast, the Freire
    label is clearly a different, 6-glyph word.
