# Battles 113-115 verification (independent, Opus)

Verifier: independent Opus reviewer, 2026-11-17. Files checked (commit bf6e7ea, branch `smaps-a`): `design/data/113.json`,
`114.json`, `115.json` and `notes-113-115.md`. No data file was edited.

**Result: 113 matches the map. 114 has 1 discrepancy:** the French leader on 8,11 is Leval, not "Laval". **115 has
1 discrepancy:** 5,0 and 5,1 should carry `crossing: "ford"`. They are the page's two straight ford tiles, hidden under
blocks. There is also a small error in a note (the bend list). Every unit, block colour, hill, forest, town, bridge,
objective and every other river hex on all three maps is correct.

## Method

- **Read the map first.** Each map (`cnc-napoleonics-research/maps/1NN.jpg`) was read hex by hex from the full image
  (block centres converted to r,c with the map's own grid) and from grid crops. Only then was the reading compared with
  the JSON, entry by entry. The reading covered every block (colour, code label, stacked leader), every terrain hex,
  the river tile seams and bend/straight shape, the bridge, town labels and objective outlines.
- **Grid.** Each file's `grid` equals the map's fit in `scripts/maps/grids.json`: 113 is (74.0, 129.88), 114 is
  (74.38, 130.62) and 115 is (72.12, 133.12, pitch 75.38, its own pitch). w is 87 and pitch 75.5 elsewhere. On all
  three maps the grid lines sit on the printed hex outlines and on the white seams between river tiles.
- **Renders.** `render.py` drew each file over its map (`map-work/verify-113-115/r11N.png/`). At full size every unit
  tag, leader tag, terrain letter, the bridge and ford bars and the objective outlines sit on the map feature they name.
- **Crops.** `crops.py --scale 2.5` with grid and r,c labels over the whole board of each map, in nine blocks of
  3 rows by 5 columns (`map-work/verify-113-115/c11N_<rows>_<cols>.png/`). Every unit cluster, hill, forest, river,
  ford, bridge, town and objective outline was checked in these crops. There are extra 3-6× plain zooms of the 114
  top-row blocks (`z114_top_mid.png`), the 114 Spanish stack (`z114_lard_area.png`) and the 115 river
  (`z115_river_left.png`, `z115_river_right.png`, `z115_r5c0-2.png`).
- **Leader labels.** The rotated side labels were cropped, turned upright and enlarged 10× (`z113_leaders.png`,
  `z113_lorcet.png`, `z114_leaders2.png`, `z114_leaders.png`, `z115_leaders.png`), with grey contrast-stretched 20×
  versions of the 114 French labels (`z114_fr_names_gray.png`, `z_leval_102_vs_114.png`). Each name was checked
  against the scenario page's historical notes (commandsandcolors.net, pages 113-115).
- **Block counts by eye** per side and per nation/code, compared with the JSON and the survey matrix.
- **check.py**: `ok` for all three files.
- **scan.py**: 113 has 0 flags. 114 flags 4,3 (hill 0.06): the crop shows a full ray ring and the dotted outline, and
  the "Barossa Ridge" caption sits on the hex centre, so it is a false flag. 115 flags 5,1, 5,3 and 6,4 (river, water
  0.01-0.02): 5,1 is mostly covered by the FR/LT block, and 5,3 and 6,4 are the lighter-blue bend-ford art that the
  teal test misses (about 775 light-teal pixels each, centred within ±20 px of the hex centre). All three are false
  flags. The `--dump` was also read for every hex. Data hills score 0.12-0.21 (4,3 on 114 excepted, as above), and
  every other hex scores at most 0.06. Forests score 0.21-0.29. Water above 0.02 appears only on data river hexes and
  on two 115 forest hexes, where it is dark green leaking into the teal test.
