Verifier: independent Opus reviewer, 2026-11-17. Files checked at commit `4472c43` (branch `maps-b`):
`design/data/010.json`, `011.json`, `012.json` and `notes-010-012.md`. No data file was edited.

- **011: 1 discrepancy.** The leader name on 1,3 is "Campbell", but the survey's chosen value is "H. Campbell".
- **010 and 012:** match. The 010 notes file has one wrong count (BR 12 instead of 10). It is a notes error only; the

- The three doubts from the transcription are settled below.
- The wording points at the end are optional.

## Method

- **Maps.** `cnc-napoleonics-research/maps/010.jpg`, `011.jpg`, `012.jpg` (1190×866). Images from this check are in
  `map-work/verify-010-012/`.
- **Grid.** Each file's `grid` equals the map's fit in `scripts/maps/grids.json`:
  - 010: 74.38, 129.88.
  - 011: 72.38, 132.38.
  - 012: 73.88, 129.88.
  - All three: w 87.0, pitch 75.5.
  - On the labelled overlays (`overlay.py`), every hex outline sits on the map's hex lines.
- **Independent reading first.** I read each map from the labelled overlay before opening the data file:
  - every block (side from the frame colour, nation from red or brown, code from the label and icon);
  - every leader label;
  - every terrain hex.
- **Crops.** The reading was then checked in 2× crops of every quadrant (`crops.py`). Closer views:
  - 4× single-hex crops of 010 3,4;
  - 6–8× rotated crops of every leader label (all 15 leaders);
  - 6–14× crops of the 012 square markers.
- **Block counts by side**, counted on the map, not taken from the file:
  - **010 top:** 10 red and 5 brown.
    - Red: LN 6 / LT 1 / RI 1 / LC 1 / FA 1.
    - Brown: LN 2 / LT 1 / HC 2.
    - Leader blocks: Campbell and Wallace (red) and Power (brown) stand behind LN blocks.
  - **010 bottom:** 13 blocks: LN 7 / LT 1 / LC 3 / FA 2. Thomières and Maucune stand alone.
  - **011 top:** 17 blocks: LN 7 / LT 1 / RI 1 / GG 2 / LC 2 / HC 2 / FA 1 / HA 1.
    - Alten and Campbell stand alone.
    - The "Brock" leader block is behind HC 1,11.
  - **011 bottom:** 14 blocks: LN 8 / LT 3 / LC 1 / FA 2. Ferey and Foy stand alone.
  - **012 top:** 10 blocks: LC 6 / HC 4. Anson is on LC 1,4, Bock on HC 2,7 and Gleichen on HC 2,10.
  - **012 bottom:** 13 blocks: LN 6 / LT 2 / LC 2 / HC 2 / FA 1. Curto is on HC 8,3 and Foy stands alone.
  - All equal the survey matrix and the data files.
- **`check.py`** on all three files: `ok` (exit 0).
- **`scan.py`**, run here. I looked at every flag in a crop. All three flags are false: each hex is clearly river.
  - **010 3,0 river (0.02):** the river runs along the east edge of the hex, and the tile seams to 2,1 and 4,1 are visible.
  - **010 4,1 river (0.02):** the bridge covers most of the water.
  - **011 6,3 river (0.02):** the river hugs the east side of the hex, between the tile seams to 5,3 and 7,3.
  - Apart from these flags, scan.py's hill, forest and water sets equal the data exactly.
  - Hills under blocks score 0.12–0.18:
    - 010: 1,4, 7,8, 7,9 and 7,10.
    - 011: 1,5 and 1,9.
  - Forest under the 011 HA on 1,10 scores 0.27.
- **Renders.** I rendered each file with `render.py` and compared it with the map at full size and in crops:
  - every unit tag sits on its block with the right code and side;
  - every leader tag sits on the block bearing that name;
  - every terrain tag sits on its art.
- **Booklet.** I extracted the schematic maps for 010–012 from `sources/CCN_Scen_Book-2012.pdf` (pages 11–13) and
  compared them hex by hex. They agree with the maps on every unit, leader and terrain hex.
  - 010: the booklet draws the brown blocks as P-HC, P-LN and P-LT, and 3,4 as a white (plain) hex.
  - 012: the booklet labels the markers "SQUARE 1" and "SQUARE 2".

- **Forest:** 0,11, and 1,10 under the HA (trees show around the block).
- **Units and leaders:** all 31 units and 5 leader positions match the map.
- **Discrepancy 1: 1,3 leader `"name": "Campbell"` should be `"H. Campbell"`.**
  - **What the sources read:** the map label at 8× reads "Campbell", with no initial. The booklet diagram (page 12)
    labels the same star "H. Campbell".
  - **Survey:** it lists "H. Campbell", and its precedence puts the booklet above the map.
  - **Rule:** the batch brief says to follow the survey's chosen value where map and survey disagree. The notes
    record the difference but do not follow it.
  - **Why it matters:** the initial also tells him apart from the Campbell in 010, who is a different officer.
  - **Fix:** set the name to "H. Campbell" and keep the note that the map label lacks the "H.".
- **Doubt settled: Brock / Bock.**
  - The 1,11 label reads "Brock" plainly at 8×. It is not ambiguous. The booklet diagram for 011 also prints "Brock".
  - The survey's chosen value is "Bock" (discrepancy 15: 012 and the booklet text say Bock; Bock is the historical
    KGL cavalry commander).
  - The file's "Bock" follows the survey, so this is **not a discrepancy**.
  - The notes' "label reads Brock/Bock" should say the label reads "Brock" (see the wording points).
  - For the controller: the survey's line "spellings follow the booklet" does not settle this case, because the booklet
    has Brock in its 011 diagram and Bock in its 012 text.
- **Objectives:** none, which is correct.
