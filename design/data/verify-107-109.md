# Battles 107–109 verification (independent, Opus)

Verifier: independent Opus reviewer, 2026-11-17. Files checked at commit `0ba7a37` (branch `smaps-a`):
`design/data/107.json`, `108.json`, `109.json` and `notes-107-109.md`. No data file was edited.

**Result: 107 and 108 match the maps. 109 has 1 discrepancy: the lone French leader on 7,8 should be named "Leval",
not "Laval".** All of the transcriber's flagged doubts are settled below. 107 hill 3,4 is confirmed. 109's Portina
Brook (5 hexes) is confirmed. 109's redoubt sides are confirmed by a pixel match with 006's identical art. Every other
leader name is confirmed.

## Method

- **Maps.** `cnc-napoleonics-research/maps/107.jpg`, `108.jpg`, `109.jpg` (1190×866). Images from this check are in
  `map-work/verify-107-109/`.
- **Grid.** Each data file's `grid` equals the map's fit in `scripts/maps/grids.json`. That is 107: 74.12, 133.0,
  pitch 75.0. 108: 72.12, 133.0, pitch 75.5. 109: 74.38, 129.88, pitch 75.5. w is 87 on all three. On the labelled
  overlays (`overlay.py`, `ov-10N.png/`), every hex outline sits on the printed hex lines, including 107's own pitch.
- **Independent reading first.** Each map was read from its labelled overlay before the JSON was opened for comparison:
  every block (side from frame colour, code from label and icon, stacks), every leader block, terrain hex and works
  wall. The reading was then checked in 2–3× crops of every unit cluster, hill block, forest, river, town and works.
  Single hexes were zoomed to 4–8×: 107 3,4, 7,2 and 8,2; the 109 redoubts; 109 8,2.
- **Block counts by side**, counted on the map and not taken from the file:
  - 107: top 14 = SP LN 8 / LT 1 / LC 2 / FA 3, plus 3 stacked leaders (with LN 2,10, LN 3,2 and FA 3,4). Bottom
    13 = FR LN 9 / LC 1 / CU 1 / FA 2, plus 3 leaders (stacked with LN 6,2; lone at 7,6 and 8,4).
  - 108: top 14 = SP LN 7 / LT 2 / GR 1 / LC 2 / FA 2, plus 2 leaders (lone at 1,7; with LN 3,7). Bottom 14 = FR
    LN 8 / LT 1 / LC 2 / CU 1 / FA 2, plus 3 leaders (with LN 6,7, LN 7,1 and CU 8,2).
  - 109: top 20. That is 16 yellow (SP LN 6 / LT 2 / GR 1 / LC 3 / HC 1 / FA 3) and 4 red (BR LN 2 / GG 1 / FA 1),
    plus 2 lone leaders (yellow 0,4, red 1,11). Bottom 17 = FR LN 7 / LT 2 / LC 1 / HC 3 / FA 3 / HA 1, plus 3
    leaders (lone 7,8; with LN 7,11; with HC 8,3).
  - All three equal the survey matrix and the data files, hex for hex.
- **`check.py`** on all three files: `ok`.
- **`scan.py`** was run here and every flag was looked at in a crop:
  - 107: 3,4 hill (0.08) is a false flag. The SP/FA and Lazan stack covers the hex. In the 3× plain crop
    (`107_r2-3_c3-7_plain.png`) the ray ring shows above and right of the stack, as on the hill hexes 3,5 and 3,6
    beside it.
  - 108: no flags.
  - 109: 2,9 hill (0.15) is a false flag: it is the redoubt wall's shadow. 2,10 scores 0.09 for the same reason.
    006's identical art scores 0.15 on 2,4.
  - Apart from these, scan.py's hill, forest and water sets equal the data. A dump listing every hex where scan.py and
    the data disagree at lower thresholds turns up only the river and works hexes discussed below.
- **Renders** (`render.py`; `107.png`, `108.png`, `109.png`) were compared at full size:
  - Every unit tag sits on its block with the right code and side colour.
  - Every leader tag sits on the block bearing that name.
  - Every terrain tag sits on its art.
  - The works sides on 109 are drawn on the walled hexsides.
- **Leader labels** are rotated block-side labels. They were read at 7–9× after rotating the image (`lbl107.png`,
  `lbl108.png`, `lbl109.png`). They were then checked against the names on the scenario pages, fetched from
  commandsandcolors.net: the historical notes and the forum comments.

## 109 Talavera (Spanish Flank) — 1 discrepancy

- **Units:** all 37 units are in the hexes the file gives, with the right codes and nations.
  - The red blocks are BR/LN 0,11 and 2,11, BR/FA 2,10 and BR/GG 2,12 (5 on its face).
  - SP/HC 0,1 is labelled HC. FR/HA 7,3 is labelled HA. FR/HC 8,3, 8,4 and 8,11 are labelled HC.
- **Leaders:**
  - Cuesta 0,4 (lone, yellow), Campbell 1,11 (lone, red), Sebastiani 7,11 (with LN) and Milhaud 8,3 (with HC) read
    clearly at 9×. All four are named on the page.
  - **DISCREPANCY — 7,8, the lone French horse block: the file says "Laval"; it should be "Leval".**
    - The label is about 5 px high. Its second letter cannot be told apart as "a" or "e": at 14–18× the 109 label is
      indistinguishable from 107's "Laval" and from 102's "Leval" (`laval-cmp.png`, `leval-cmp.png`).
    - The scenario page's historical notes name **Leval** five times. Leval's division attacks through the trees onto
      the Spanish batteries at Pajar de Vergara, which is exactly this setup.
    - The page never mentions Laval. Laval (107) commanded a division in Aragon under Suchet, not at Talavera.
    - Name to use: `"Leval"`.
- **Town:** 0,2, 0,3 and 1,3 (Talavera). This equals the page's Town 3.
- **Forest (8):** 3,2, 4,0, 4,5, 5,6, 5,8, 6,4, 6,10 and 7,4 (FR/LT on it). Scan.py gives them forest 0.18–0.22, the
  page says Forest 8, and there are no others.
- **Portina Brook (5) — confirmed:**
  - 0,9 is a bend from the top edge into the hex's E side (SP/LC on it). The water is inside 0,9's upper half.
  - 0,10 is a bend, W to SE, carrying the label.
  - 1,10 is straight, NW to SE.
  - 2,11 is straight, NW to SE (BR/LN on it).
  - 3,11 is a bend, NW to the right board edge (E).
  - This equals the page's River 2 + bend 3. None of them has a crossing, which matches the controller's ruling: the
    brook is plain river, and the brook rule is scenario text. No water is in 1,11, 0,11, 2,12 or 3,10.
- **Redoubts (Pajar de Vergara) — sides confirmed: 2,9 W+SW+SE and 2,10 SW+SE+E, both on plain.**
  - A brown V shows below both blocks, on the SW and SE sides of each hex (6× crop `109_r2-2_c9-10_plain.png`).
  - The vertical walls were measured as luminance and brown-pixel profiles across dx ±25–44 from the hex centre,
    along dy −15 to +20. They were compared with 006 2,4 and 2,5 (W and E walls, confirmed in verify-004-006) and with
    the plain-ground FA blocks 109 2,1 and 2,4:
    - **2,9, left side:** brown wall at dx −33/−32, then a shadow band 140, 116, 108, 107, 118, 106 at dx −39 to −34.
      This is identical to 006 2,4 within 1–2 levels.
    - **2,9, right side:** bright meadow (152–157). There is no E wall.
    - **2,10, right side:** wall at dx +32, then a shadow band 94, 114, 104, 95, 107, 129, 145 at dx +33 to +39. This
      is identical to 006 2,5 (94, 114, 103, 97, 106, 128, 147).
    - **2,10, left side:** meadow (161–163). There is no W wall.
    - The control blocks 2,1 and 2,4 show plain meadow on both sides.
  - Nothing brown is above either block, so there are no NW or NE walls.
  - No ray ring is visible and the survey has no hills in 109, so both are `on: plain`.
- **Objectives:** none (correct).
