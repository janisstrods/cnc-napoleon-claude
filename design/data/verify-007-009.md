# Battle maps 007–009 verification

Independent verifier (Opus), 2026-11-17. Files checked: `design/data/007.json`, `008.json`, `009.json` and
`notes-007-009.md` at commit b572349 (branch `maps-a`). No data file was edited.

**Result: all 3 battles match their maps and the survey. 0 discrepancies.** The transcriber's three doubts are settled
below (hill 009 4,4 is a hill; the 009 6,2 river-with-bridge is correct; the blurred label reads Mermet).

## Method

- **Maps:** `cnc-napoleonics-research/maps/007.jpg`, `008.jpg`, `009.jpg` (1190×866). Renders and crops are in
  `map-work/verify-007-009/`.
- **Grid:** each file's `grid` equals the per-map fit in `scripts/maps/grids.json`: 007 (72.38, 132.38), 008 (72.25,
  132.38), 009 (74.38, 130.38), all w 87.0 and pitch 75.5.
- **Own reading first:** before reading the data, I placed every block, town, forest, hill and river tile on the bare
  maps from the hex-centre formula. Then I compared that list with the files.
- **Renders:** `render.py` full size for each battle. Then 2–3× render or plain crops of every unit cluster, hill block,
  forest, town and river, read hex by hex. Block type labels, side colour (red BR, brown PT, blue FR) and the
  LIGHT/HEAVY/FOOT/HORSE bands were read on plain crops.
- **Leader labels:** read on the stack sides at 5–6× (rotated to read).
- **Counts:** blocks per side and per key counted on the maps, independently of the files, and compared with survey §2.1.
- **Tools:**
  - `check.py` on all three files: ok, exit 0.
  - `scan.py`: 007 0 flags, 008 0 flags, 009 2 flags (4,4 hill 0.11; 6,2 river 0.02). Both 009 flags were checked in
    crops (below).
  - `scan.py --dump` on all three: hill hexes 0.13–0.18 (except 009 4,4); non-hill hexes 0.00–0.06, including every
    unit hex the data calls plain. Forest hexes 0.22–0.29, all others ≤ 0.03. Water appears only on the data's river
    hexes. No terrain on any map is missing from a file.

### Doubts settled

- **Hill 4,4 (scan 0.11): hill, correct.**
  - In the 3× plain crop, hill rays show above and below Mermet's stack.
  - The hex has the same light hill-tile outline as 4,5 and 4,6.
  - Plain unit hexes on this map scan 0.00, so 0.11 is a hill partly covered by a two-block stack, not noise.
- **River with bridge 6,2 (scan 0.02): correct.**
  - The bridge tile sits on 6,2, and the river runs diagonally through that hex, from 5,1 (NW side) to 7,2 (SE side).
  - The bridge deck covers most of the water, which explains the low fraction.
  - No river touches 5,2, 6,1 or 6,3.
  - `crossing: "bridge"` matches the page's "River with bridge 1" and the impassable-except-at-the-bridge rule.
- **Mermet's label: reads "Mermet".**
  - At 4× and 6×, the label is six letters, with the shape M-e-r-m-e-t; set beside Marchand and Loison on the same map,
    "Mermet" fits it.
  - General Mermet commanded a division in Ney's corps in this campaign. The survey's spelling stands.
