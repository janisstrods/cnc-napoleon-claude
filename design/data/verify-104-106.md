# Battles 104-106 verification (independent, Opus)

Verifier: independent Opus reviewer, 2026-11-17. Files checked (commit debbb23, branch `smaps-b`): `design/data/104.json`,
`105.json`, `106.json` and `notes-104-106.md`. No data file was edited.

**Result: every unit, leader position and terrain hex on all three maps matches the files. There are 3 findings:**

1. **105 leader 8,7: name.** The map reads **"Ruffin"**. The file has `"Rufin?"`.
2. **104 Shallow Stream: `crossing: "ford"` on 5,10 6,11 7,11 8,12.** This breaks the way fordable rivers are recorded
   everywhere else, and it would give the stream the movement rule of a ford tile. Recommend removing it (see 104).
3. **105 notes: block counts.** The note lists two units as non-standard that are standard: SP/LT 1,8 (5) and FR/GA
   8,3 (3). Only the notes are wrong; the data is not affected.

## Method

- **Read the map first.** Each map (`maps/104.png`; `105.jpg`, `106.jpg`) was read hex by hex from 2× grid crops in
  nine 3-row × 5-column blocks per map (`map-work/verify-104-106/c<id>-<rows>-<cols>.png/`). The independent reading
  covered every block (side colour, code label, block number, stack) and every terrain hex. It was then compared with
  the JSON entry by entry.
- **Grid.** Each file's `grid` equals its `grids.json` fit (104 and 105: 74.38, 130.38; 106: 74.0, 129.88; w 87,
  pitch 75.5). The grid lines sit on the printed hex outlines in every crop.
- **Renders.** `render.py` drew each file over its map (`verify-104-106/r10N.png/`). There is also a 4× render of the
  105 redoubt (`rz105/`).
- **Zooms.** 4× crops of the 104 stream source (`z104a`) and the 104 hill under the HA (`z104b`). A 6× crop of the
  105 redoubt (`z105_53.png`). 8-20× rotated crops of every leader label on 105 and 106 (`z105_81r`, `z105_87r`,
  `z105_lab*`, `z105_17`, `z105_310`, `z106_labels.png`). 104's labels are legible at 1×.
- **Block counts** were made by eye per side and code. They equal the files and the survey matrix: 104 SP 17 + 3 and
  FR 17 + 3; 105 SP 17 + 3 and FR 15 + 2; 106 SP 16 + 3 and FR 15 + 4.
- **check.py:** `ok` on all three.
- **scan.py** flags 3 hexes, all false:
  - 104: none.
  - 105 5,3 (hill 0.15): the redoubt's earth wall and its shadow, on a plain hex.
  - 106 1,9 (hill 0.12): rays are visible round the FA.
  - 106 8,9 (water 0.02): the bridge covers the river.
- **scan.py --dump, whole board.** It was compared with each file at looser thresholds (hill ≥ 0.08, forest ≥ 0.08,
  water ≥ 0.02):
  - No hex outside the data scores as hill, forest or water.
  - Data hills score 0.12-0.19 and data forests 0.16-0.26.
  - The only 104 hits are forest hexes whose dark trees spill into the hill class.
- **Scenario pages.** The historical notes of the 104, 105 and 106 pages were fetched to cross-check leader names.

- **Finding 2: the ford marking.**
  - The map draws the Shallow Stream as an ordinary river with no ford tile or ford patch. It is fordable only by the
    page rule ("The Shallow Stream is fordable").
  - Every other battle records rule-fordable water with no `crossing`: 001, 003, 004, 007-009 and 015 (notes and
    verifications).

  - The rules also differ. A fordable stream stops a unit on entry. A ford tile does not: survey 115 says "crossing does

  - **Recommendation:** remove `crossing` from 5,10 6,11 7,11 8,12. Leave the Trueba unmarked as well (impassable by
    rule).
    - The two water rules belong in the scenario rules, keyed by river. The `art` labels ("River Trueba" and
      "Shallow Stream") already name the two rivers.
    - The file note should give each river's hex list and rule. It does this already; only the "carries crossing
      ford" sentence needs to change, in both the file's `notes` and `notes-104-106.md`.
