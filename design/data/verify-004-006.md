# Battles 004–006 verification (independent, Opus)

Verifier: independent Opus reviewer, 2026-11-17. Files checked at commit `d060b8f` (branch `maps-b`):
`design/data/004.json`, `005.json`, `006.json` and `notes-004-006.md`. No data file was edited.

**Result: all 3 battles match the maps. 0 discrepancies.** The three doubts from the transcription are settled below:
005's works sides SW, SE, E are confirmed by new evidence, 006's works sides are confirmed, and 006's leader label reads
"Sydney". The recommendations at the end are optional note edits; none of them is a discrepancy.

## Method

- **Maps.** `cnc-napoleonics-research/maps/004.jpg`, `005.jpg`, `006.jpg` (1190×866). Images from this check are in
  `map-work/verify-004-006/`.
- **Grid.** Each data file's `grid` equals the map's fit in `scripts/maps/grids.json` (004: 72.38, 132.38; 005: 72.25,
  132.38; 006: 74.38, 129.88; w 87.0, pitch 75.5). On the labelled overlays (`overlay.py`) every hex outline sits on the
  map's hex lines.
- **Independent reading first.** Each map was read from the labelled overlay before the data file was compared: every
  block (side from frame colour, code from the label and icon), leader label, terrain hex and works side. The reading
  was then checked in 2× crops of every quadrant (`crops.py`) and in 3–8× single-hex crops of every hill under a block,
  river end, junction, bridge, town, fortress, windmill and works hex.
- **Block counts by side** (counted on the map, not taken from the file):
  - 004: top 12 units + Moore alone; bottom 15. LN 6 / LT 2 / RI 1 / GG 1 / LC 1 / FA 1 and LN 8 / LT 3 / HC 2 / FA 2.
  - 005: top 16 units + 3 leader blocks alone; bottom 19. LN 6 / LT 1 / RI 1 / GG 1 / LC 3 / GHC 1 / FA 3 and
    LN 10 / LT 3 / FA 3 / HC 1 / LC 2.
  - 006: top 13 units (11 red, 2 brown) + Craufurd alone; bottom 16 + Loison alone. LN 3 / LT 1 / RI 2 / LC 1 / HC 1 /
    FA 1 / HA 2 + PT/LT 2 and LN 6 / LT 3 / GR 1 / LC 3 / HC 2 / FA 1.
  - All equal the survey and the data files.
- **`check.py`** on all three files: `ok` (exit 0).
- **`scan.py`**, run here; every flag was looked at in a crop:
  - 004: 2,2 river (0.02) and 4,3 river (0.01). False flags. On 2,2 the river runs along the east half of the hex, and on
    4,3 the Y junction sits at the hex centre, inside the area scan.py skips for blocks. Both are clearly river.
  - 005: 2,0 hill (0.08). False flag. The FA block and the works cover most of the hex. Faint rays show around the block.
  - 006: 0,4 river (0.02). False flag: the bridge covers most of the water. 2,4 hill (0.15) is the dark shadow of the
    works walls; the crop shows no ray ring, and the booklet draws 2,4 and 2,5 as entrenchment tiles, not hills.
  - Apart from these flags, scan.py's hill, forest and water sets equal the data exactly in all three battles. Hill and
    forest hexes under blocks score 0.13–0.19 and 0.21–0.28.
- **Renders** (`render.py`, full size, plus crops of the works hexes): every unit tag sits on its block with the right
  code and side. Every leader tag sits on the block bearing that name. Every terrain tag sits on its art, and the works
  sides are drawn on the walled hexsides.
- **Booklet.** The schematic maps for 004–006 were extracted from `sources/CCN_Scen_Book-2012.pdf` (pages 5–7) with
  pypdf; no file was executed. They agree with the maps on every unit, leader and terrain hex compared.

    - A dark, non-brown band runs from dx +35 to +39 along dy −18 to +21. Its luminance is 93–120, against 155–165
      for the meadow, and it fades out at +40/+41.
    - On 006's 2,5 the E wall's drop shadow has the same profile: brown at +27 to +32, a dark band at +33 to +39, fading
      at +40/+41, along dy −15 to +21.

      works' shadow, not the block's shadow or hill shading.

    shadow. A W wall would show there, as it does on 006's 2,4.
