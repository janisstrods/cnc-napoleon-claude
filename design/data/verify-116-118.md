## Method

- **Read the map first.** Each map (`maps/116.jpg`, `117.jpg`, `118.jpg`) was read hex by hex from 3× grid crops,
  nine 3-row × 5-column blocks per map (`map-work/verify-116-118/c<id>-<rows>-<cols>/`). The reading covered side
  colour, nation colour, code label, stack/leader and terrain for every hex. It was then compared with the JSON entry
  by entry.
- **Grid.** Each file's `grid` equals its `grids.json` fit (116: 72.5, 132.38; 117: 74.38, 130.62; 118: 74.38, 130.38;
  w 87, pitch 75.5). Grid lines sit on the printed hex outlines in every crop (`ov<id>.png/`).
- **Renders.** `render.py` drew each file over its map (`r<id>.png/`). Every tag sits on its map feature.
- **Zooms:**
  - 4× crops of the 117 centre outline (`z117centre/`, `z117centrep/`).
  - 5× crops of the 118 works hexes and of 7,10 and 8,10 (`hz118_*.png`).
  - 8× and 20× nearest-neighbour crops of 8,10 (`z118_810_nearest.png`, `z118_810_speck.png`), and an 8× crop of the
    116 1,10 bridge for comparison (`z116_110_bridge.png`).
  - 9× rotated crops of every leader label (`labels116/117/118.png`).
- **Block counts** were made by eye per side and nation. They equal the files and the survey matrix: 116 BR 13 + 2,
  PT 5 + 1, SP 4 + 1, FR 20 + 4; 117 SP 13 + 3, FR 15 + 3; 118 BR 9 + 2, SP 7 + 1, FR 16 + 3.
- **check.py:** `ok` on all three.
- **scan.py** flags 7 hexes. None of them is a data error:
  - 116 1,10 (water 0.02): the bridge art covers the bend.
  - 117 2,5 (hill 0.11): rays show round the Losada stack.
  - 118 2,3 (hill 0.11): rays show round the Reeves stack.
  - 118 3,4, 4,9 and 5,3 (hill 0.07-0.09): the entrenchment art covers part of the ray ring.
  - 118 8,10 (water 0.02): the LC block covers the river.
- **scan.py --dump, whole board.** It was compared with each file at looser thresholds (water ≥ 0.02, forest ≥ 0.08,
  hill ≥ 0.08). No hex outside the data scores as water, forest or hill.
- **Scenario pages.** The pages for 116, 117 and 118 were fetched for their terrain lists, special rules and history.

### Finding 1: the bridge is at 8,10

- **The page.** Its terrain list is River 1, River Bend 1, River Bridge 1, and its history has Suchet's troops crossing
  "the Bridge of Lledoner". With three river hexes, 7,11 is the River, 7,10 the River Bend, and 8,10 must be the River
  Bridge tile. 8,10 is a straight hex, as a River Bridge tile is, so the map has no extra river tile beyond the page's
  three.
- **The map.** A dark grey wedge about 3 × 6 px sticks out from under the left edge of the FR/LC block at 8,10, at
  about (−27, −10) from the hex centre (pixels #3b4141 and #727a62, on grass).
  - The colour matches the dark outline of the bridge art on 116.
  - No other block has such a mark: the identical FR/LC at 8,9 has clean grass at the same offset.
  - In 8,10 the river runs NE-SW, so a bridge would lie WNW-ESE, about 45 px long. Its WNW end would fall just where the
    wedge is, and the rest would be hidden by the 64 px block.
- **Recommendation:** set `crossing: "bridge"` on 8,10, and record the hidden art in `art`, for example `"bridge under
  the FR/LC block; west end visible"`. This also meets the survey (River with bridge 1).
