# C&C: Napoleonics — Plan 5: AI Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the seeded random AI stub with a computer general that plays Commands & Colours: Napoleonics well at three difficulty levels: squares, artillery range, the shrinking dice of worn infantry, Retire and Reform, Combined Arms, First Strike, objectives and the card hand included. It is calibrated by logged AI-vs-AI runs over the 15 base battles, and **the plan ends with the base game playable locally**.

**Architecture:** This is the Ancients edition's proven design (git `bf3a648^:src/ai/*`), rebuilt on the Napoleonics engine. Each card decision runs a Monte-Carlo turn planner: candidate (card, orders, moves) plans are played out with the engine's own `turnFlow` on *determinised* clones, scored by a static evaluation, and compared, minus the value of keeping the card. The battle phase uses rollouts. Reactive decisions use fast expected-value policies built on an analytic probability model. That model gets its dice counts, hit faces, terrain, flags and retreats **from engine calls** (`fireDice`, `meleeDice`, `scoreRoll`, `flagIgnores`, `retreatOptions`, `defendOptions`, …), never from copies of rules. One module, `src/ai/view.ts`, is the hidden-information boundary. The new modules are built and tested beside the stub. `chooseAnswer` switches to them in one late task, so other plans' tests stay fast until then.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 5.6, Vite 5, Vitest 2, Web Worker (`src/ai/worker.ts`, unchanged protocol). Scripts run with `npx vite-node`.

**Spec:** `design/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-cnc-napoleonics-design.md` (Goals; Anti-goals: "an AI that is mechanically legal but plays C&C:N like Ancients"; Look and feel: difficulty names and commanders; phase 6). Rules: `design/rules-reference.md` (RR), `design/rulings.md`. Roadmap: Plan 1. Engine contract: `design/superpowers/plans/2026-11-17-plan-2b-engine.md` (module map, frozen contract) and `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-17-plan-2b-engine/{carry-forward.md,waves-ownership.md}`. Ancients reference: `git show bf3a648^:src/ai/<file>.ts` (planner, sim, estimate, evaluate, cardsense, values, ordering, moves, policies, voice, board, dice) and `git show bf3a648^:scripts/ai-{balance,balance-report,review,match}.ts`; lessons in `design/ancients/exp{1,2,3}-ai-balance.md`.

## What carries over from Ancients, and what does not

**Carried over** (ported in shape, rewritten for these rules):
- plan at the card decision, then follow the stored plan for `orders` and `move`;
- determinise with a canonical sort before shuffling, so the result depends only on *which* cards are unseen;
- common random seeds across candidates, paired close-call tests (2.5 standard errors), successive screening (`k0` → `kMax`) and a leftover-budget top-up;
- battle rollouts per candidate attack;
- the weakest level's evaluation noise, softmax pick, mistake rate and hasty (simulation-free) turns;
- the forced-dice guard and `safeAnswer` after an engine refusal in a simulation;
- banner steps that rise towards victory;
- personality-derived weights.

**Not ported, measured useless in Ancients:**
- a 2-ply opponent reply (`lookahead`);
- local plan refinement (`refine`);
- the exact-movement threat model (`exactEval`, 3× slower, no gain). The threat model uses distance-based reach as Ancients' `threatsAgainst`/`reachOf` did, cross-checked by sampling against `moveOptions` (Task 5);
- the datagen / logistic-fit scripts. Port `ai-datagen`/`ai-fit` from `bf3a648^` only if Task 11's strength target fails and the hand-set weights cannot be fixed by inspection.

**Lesson kept:** temperament moves balance strongly. A cautious temperament on the side that must attack loses lopsidedly (Ancients' Darius as the Shield). Task 11 checks each battle's attacker temperament.

## Review Focus

1. **The worker dies mid-turn** (timeout, then an in-thread fallback with fresh memory) **or an autosave is reloaded during the AI's turn.** The AI must answer `orders`/`move`/`battle` from the state as it is, never replay a stored plan on a changed state. Pinned by Task 9's `index.test.ts` "a stored plan whose fingerprint differs is dropped" and "fresh memory at a move decision answers legally".
2. **The opponent cannot form square** (hand of 2 or fewer, track full, town hex). The AI's cavalry must see that the target is now soft, and its own infantry must not count on a square it cannot form. Pinned by Task 4a's `reactions.test.ts` "square odds follow the engine's own conditions" (hand 4 against hand 2, town hex).
3. **A passive stand-off:** both AIs hold good ground and nobody attacks (012's British cavalry facing French squares is the likeliest case). Every game must still end. Pinned by Task 9's `battles-*.slow.test.ts` (every battle ends by turn 150) and Task 5's `evaluate.test.ts` "advance pull breaks a stand-off (012)".
4. **A slow device or busy tab:** the strongest level must stop at its deadline with a legal answer even when evaluation is slow, and plan building must count against the same budget. Huge candidate counts (Leadership with many leaders, Cold Steel with many adjacent units) must not explode. Pinned by Task 8's `planner.test.ts` "stops at the deadline under an injected slow clock" and "plan building counts against the node budget", and by Task 6a's `ordering.test.ts` "candidate count is capped".
5. **A hidden card leaking** through commentary, a reaction, or a clone made outside the boundary: a `say` line naming a card still in hand, a First Strike guess read from the real hand, or a stray `cloneState` that keeps the opponent's real hand. Pinned by Task 9's `voice.test.ts` "lines never name a card not yet played", Task 1's `hidden-scan.test.ts` (dot, bracket, destructuring and `cloneState`) and Task 9's `hidden-info.slow.test.ts` (re-dealt variants of every decision kind).

**Value of an outcome.** The value is Σ P(h, f) over:
- min(h, blocks) × the block values lost;
- an elimination's banner (`bannersWorth`);
- the flag result: blocks lost to a blocked retreat, or a displacement cost of 0.07 per hex retreated;
- the attached leader's casualty check × leader value.

Then subtract the expected battle back: the defender's `meleeDice` in role `'back'`, applied to the attacker when the defender survives in place.

- Produces:

    - otherwise: deadline `budgetMs × scale` (× 0.3 when `battle`), with the node cap still applying.

**Starting `LEVELS`** (rows 0/1/2 = captain/colonel/marshal; the Ancients Recruit/Tribune/Consul values):

| | budgetMs | maxSims | orderCands | moveVariants | topCards | k0 | kMax | evalNoise | pickTemp | battleRollouts | battleCands | battleNoise | mistakeRate | extraVariants | topUp | hastyShare |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 150 | 90 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 4 | 0.6 | 0.3 | 0 | 0 | 0.3 | 0.3 | 0 | 0 | 0.3 |
| 1 | 800 | 1200 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 16 | 0 | 0 | 24 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 48 | 0 |
| 2 | 2000 | 5000 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 12 | 64 | 0 | 0 | 32 | 6 | 0 | 0 | 16 | 64 | 0 |

**`planTurn` algorithm.** Every evaluation, simulated turn and rollout charges `ctx.budget`, and every loop exits on `ctx.budget.spent()`. Plan building (candidate generation, move scoring) charges the same budget. The planner never calls `now()`.
1. If `forcedDiceLeft() > 0`, or a hasty turn (`rand.chance(hastyShare)`), pick by `quickCardScore − keepValue` with no simulation.
2. Screen every card: inspect its effective kind and order candidates, then build a plan for the first candidate in the base movement style.
3. Sample each plan `k0` times. Each sample uses the common seed for its index: `blindClone` → `determinize` → play card, orders and moves → battle phase by greedy policy → draw phase → `evaluate(judgeW)` with `ec.determinised = true`.
4. Score = mean − `keepValue(card) × W.retention` + `handFloorPenalty`.
5. Widen the `topCards` best cards to `orderCands × moveVariants` plans (+ `extraVariants` noisy plans for the two leaders).
6. Successive halving up to `kMax`, then top up the close rivals (paired difference < 2.5 standard errors) until the budget or `topUp` runs out.
7. Level 0: add Gaussian `evalNoise` to the scores and pick by softmax at `pickTemp`.
8. If the budget runs out before every card has `k0` samples, the screened cards keep their partial means and unscreened ones their quick score. The answer is always legal.

**`decideBattle`:** the greedy candidates by estimate. With `battleRollouts > 0` and no forced dice, the top `battleCands` attacks plus `endBattle` are rolled out (the rest of the battle phase and the draw, determinised, common seeds), and the best mean wins. Each rollout is one node of the battle budget (`maxSims × 0.3` in deterministic mode), so rollouts count in `sims`.
