# C&C: Napoleonics Expansion #1, The Spanish Army (101–118): rulings

Compiled 2026-11-17 for the browser edition. Scope: the Spanish units, the Swiss of battle 101, the French lancers and
Guard light cavalry that the expansion battles use, the Spanish Guerrilla Action mechanic, the new terrain tiles, and
the special rules of battles 101–118 as recorded in `design/exp1-survey.md`. It builds on `design/rulings.md` (R1–R72
on main, R73–R86 now merged to main with the same numbering, cited where they matter). Rules text is paraphrased
throughout; the few quotations are under 15 words.

**The decisions table is binding**, exactly as in `design/rulings.md`: `design/rules-reference.md` §15 onward cites
these ids, and engine tests cite them too. A row marked `[Interp]` is our own decision where no ranked source settles
the point; its last column gives the one-sentence reason. A row without `[Interp]` is settled by the source in its
basis column. Every base ruling (R1…) applies to the expansion unless a row here changes it; S40 lists the changes.

## Source ranking (binding)

The ranking of `design/rulings.md`, with the Expansion #1 rules and the Spanish unit card beside the 4th-edition
rulebook (plan Global Constraints). When sources disagree the higher one wins unless a row explains otherwise.

1. GMT *Rules of Play, 4th Edition* [RB4], and the Expansion #1 rules [EX1] with the Spanish unit card ([QRC] p.1, the
   only copy of the card obtained).
2. The [QRC]'s other tables and the [SITE] unit and terrain pages, which transcribe the cards and tiles.
3. The Expansion #1 scenarios [SCN1] (the battle pages, which transcribe the booklet) and their maps [MAP].
4. Richard Borg's rulings in the commandsandcolors.net FAQ [FAQ]. A designer ruling counts as errata where it narrows
   or clarifies a rule; it does not add a new effect over a higher source.
5. BoardGameGeek [BGG], including Borg answers relayed there (marked "Borg, relayed").

Later GMT texts ([GB], the Epic and Grand Battles clarifications) never override a ranked source for a two-player
Expansion #1 battle. Base controller rule R15 holds: a card "–" or a 0 base result means the attack cannot be made, and
no card creates it.

## Decisions table

Codes are the plan's fixed codes plus the survey's new **SWI** (Swiss line infantry, a distinct type for both sides,
`SP/SWI` and `FR/SWI`). "b" is the unit's current blocks; "⌊ ⌋" rounds down. "The Allied side" is the top army of each
battle (Spanish, or British with Spanish and Portuguese contingents); "the French" are always the bottom army.

| S19 | What a successful action does | The French card is discarded (it counts as the card the French played that turn), no unit or leader is ordered, and no battle happens. Cancelled **Élan**: the reshuffle of deck and discard pile still happens before the draw (R53). Cancelled **Rally**: no dice are rolled and no blocks return. Cancelled **Short Supply**: no unit is moved. | [EX1] (card discarded, no orders, French draw a new card, which ends the turn); [FAQ] Command Cards and Condensed (Borg: a cancelled Scout gives a single draw); Borg ConsimWorld clarification relayed on the [SITE] mechanic page (cancellation removes orders only; the Élan reshuffle still happens; Rally's roll is cancelled); [FAQ] Guerrilla Tokens (Borg, 2 Nov 2025: a success cancels only the orders, and end-of-turn actions still happen; for Scout the expansion's own "draws a new Command card" outranks it). | — |
| S20 | Gaining counters with Scout | Each time the Allied player plays a Scout (including a Counter-Attack that becomes a Scout), he **may** in the Draw phase draw 1 card and take 1 counter from the pool instead of drawing 2 and keeping 1. Holding is limited only by the 6 counters in the box. | [EX1] New Game Mechanic ("may acquire"; one card instead of two); BGG 2267404 (a Counter-Attack copy of Scout gains a counter); R51. | `[Interp]` for the cap: counters are taken from the pool and the box holds six; BGG answers (1792824, 2908879) say there is no limit, which only matters past six counters. |

## Source conflicts and how they were ruled

- **Spanish militia blocks (S4).** The [QRC] prints 3; [EX1], the maps and Borg say 4, and the card's own dice columns
  run to 4. Ruled 4: the designer called the card cell an error.
- **Spanish artillery retreat (S6).** The first-printing booklet (and [ES], which renders a Spanish-language edition)
  gives 2 hexes; Borg ruled the booklet wrong, and the current [EX1] transcription and the card give 1. Ruled 1.
- **Spanish light infantry moving fire (S2).** [ES] adds a −1; [EX1] and the [QRC] do not. Ruled for [EX1] and the
  card.
- **Counter-Attack after a cancelled card (S21).** The dated [FAQ] ruling (Borg, July 2015) allows the copy; an undated
  ConsimWorld note relayed on the site says not. Ruled for the [FAQ].
- **Scout cancelled by Guerrilla (S19).** The [FAQ] Scout entry gives the French a single draw; the [FAQ] Guerrilla
  Tokens entry (Borg, 2 Nov 2025) says end-of-turn actions still happen. Ruled for the single draw: the expansion's own
  text, that the French draw a new Command card, outranks any FAQ entry, and the Scout entry agrees with it. The
  2025 entry is followed for the Élan reshuffle, which the expansion text does not address.
- **Walled Farm flag-ignore (S22).** An unattributed "update" on the site and in the FAQ adds a flag-ignore; [EX1] says
  town. Ruled for [EX1]; unused in 101–118, so the choice is data-only and would be one line to change.
- **Guerrilla cap (S20).** BGG answers say there is no limit; ruled that the six counters in the box are the limit,
  since counters come from the pool. It cannot matter before a seventh counter.
