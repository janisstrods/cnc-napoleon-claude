# C&C: Napoleonics Expansion #1 (The Spanish Army): scenario survey (battles 101-118)

Compiled 2026-11-17. Sources: the 18 scenario pages on commandsandcolors.net (page tables and special rules, read from the page HTML), the 18 map images (`images/CCN/scenarios/1NN.jpg`, except 104, whose page now embeds `104.png`, modified 5 February 2026; the older `104.jpg` is out of date and kept only for reference, saved outside the repo in `cnc-napoleonics-research/maps/`), and the site's Expansion #1 pages (contents, introduction, new units and leaders, new game mechanic, new terrain tiles). The Expansion #1 scenario booklet and unit reference cards are not available online; no PDF was found on the site, so booklet comparison was not possible (see section 3). This file is the source for the battle transcription of 101-118 and for the Spanish rulings. It holds no hex coordinates; those come later from the map images.

## How to read this

- **Codes.** Nations: FR French, BR British, PT Portuguese, AL Allied (not used here), SP Spanish. Infantry: LN line, LT light, RI rifle, GR grenadier, GG Guard grenadier, ML militia. Cavalry: LC light, LNC lancer, HC heavy, CU cuirassier, GLC Guard light. Artillery: FA foot, HA horse, GA Guard foot. Leader: LDR. Units are written nation/code, e.g. SP/LN. One code is new and flagged in section 2.5: **SWI** (Swiss line infantry, battle 101 only, written SP/SWI and FR/SWI).
- **Page code translations.** The pages use LI for line infantry (108), RL for rifle, MI for militia, GFA for Guard foot artillery and LNCR for the lancer. They are translated to LN, RI, ML, GA and LNC here. Spanish units are the yellow blocks, French the dark blue; Swiss and allied blocks are described per battle.
- **Top and bottom.** The map banner names the top army and the bottom army. In every battle the top army is the Spanish side (101-113, 117, 118; the British are with them in 109 and 118) or the British side (114-116, with Spanish and Portuguese units inside it), and the French are always at the bottom. The page table lists the top army first, which agrees. Who moves first is a start icon beside the army box on the page; the map carries no marker. The French move first everywhere except 106 and 108 (Spanish) and 116 (decided by cards).
- **Hands.** Each map shows two portraits with two numbers: command cards then Tactician cards. They match the page everywhere (checked on all 18). Only the first number is the hand size; Tactician cards are optional.
- **Unit counts.** The page table was parsed from the page HTML (the icon file names, not the alt text) and compared with my own count of every map image. Each entry gives non-leader units and leaders separately for each side. Where page and map differ the map is followed (no booklet to consult), and the entry says so.
- **Leaders.** Leaders are the horse-mounted lone blocks and the taller blocks stacked behind a unit. Names on the maps are rotated and small; only those I could read with confidence are written, and they are marked "as read". "Commander" is the page text name.
- **Terrain counts** are the page counts, read from the HTML. I checked the maps by eye for the main features and found no difference except where noted. They are not hex-exact; placement comes with the transcription.
- **Block counts** shown on the maps (the number on each block face) are the reference-card values except where noted in section 2.5; the Spanish card differs from the base-game nations (see 2.5).
- "Not stated" means the source does not say. Banner effects that the text leaves ambiguous are listed as open rulings questions (section 2.6) and are not decided here.

## 1. Battles

### 101 Bailén (19 July 1808)
(Page category: Peninsular War, Expansion #1.)
- **Top:** Spanish (yellow blocks, brown Portuguese blocks for the Swiss). Commander (page): Castaños. **5 cards** (Tactician 3).
- **Bottom:** French (blue blocks, red British blocks for the Swiss). Commander (page): Dupont. **5 cards** (Tactician 2). **Moves first.**
- **Leaders (map):**
  - Top: SP: Castaños's army has one leader stacked with the Spanish GR and one with a brown Swiss LN (the Swiss leader). Names illegible at map resolution.
  - Bottom: FR: two leaders stacked with FR LN (Chabert's brigade and Dupont's) and one with a red Swiss LN.
- **Victory:** 7 banners.
- **Units (page table equals map unless noted):**
  - Top: SP/LN 5, SP/LT 2, SP/GR 1, SP/LC 2, SP/HC 2, SP/FA 2, SP/SWI 2, SP/LDR 2. **Total 16 units + 2 leaders.**
  - Bottom: FR/LN 4, FR/GR 1, FR/ML 2, FR/LC 2, FR/CU 1, FR/HC 1, FR/FA 2, FR/SWI 4, FR/LDR 3. **Total 17 units + 3 leaders.**
  - Page vs map: No differences in rows or totals (page code `MI` read as ML; `LI` is not used here).
- **Terrain (page counts):** Forest 7, Hill 15, River 2, River bend 3, River end 1, River junction (Y) 1.
- **Guerrilla Action:** Rule in effect; Spanish start with **0** counters. French side: none.
- **Objectives:** None beyond kill banners.
- **Special rules (paraphrased):**
  - The whole Rumblar River is fordable.
  - Spanish Guerrilla Action in effect, Spanish start with no counters.
  - Swiss infantry (new unit type): French Swiss are 4 LN-style units drawn as red British blocks with a British leader; Spanish Swiss are 2 units drawn as brown Portuguese blocks with a Portuguese leader. All have 5 blocks. Swiss that move and shoot roll half their blocks rounded down; they melee at 1 die per block and retreat 1 hex per flag. The page does not say how many dice Swiss infantry roll when firing while standing still.
  - Once two French Swiss units are eliminated, the other two are removed at once; their removal gives the Spanish no banners.
- **Layout (indicative):** Spanish (top) hold a high line in two groups left and right with the river down the left edge; the Swiss pair sits top-left. French (bottom) have the red Swiss line bottom-left and the main column strung along the bottom two rows. The banner track shows 7.
- **New needs:**
  - Swiss infantry unit type and its rules (SP/SWI, FR/SWI).
  - Rule: remove surviving French Swiss after two losses, no banners.
  - Y river junction and river end tiles.

(Page category: Peninsular War, Expansion #1.)
- **Top:** Spanish (yellow blocks). Commander (page): Gregorio de la Cuesta. **6 cards** (Tactician 2). **Moves first.**
- **Bottom:** French (blue blocks). Commander (page): Victor. **5 cards** (Tactician 3).

  - Bottom: FR: four leaders: three stacked with LN and one lone horse block.
- **Victory:** 6 banners.
- **Units (page table equals map unless noted):**
  - Top: SP/LN 6, SP/LT 1, SP/GR 2, SP/LC 3, SP/HC 1, SP/FA 3, SP/LDR 3. **Total 16 units + 3 leaders.**
  - Bottom: FR/LN 6, FR/LT 3, FR/LC 2, FR/HC 1, FR/FA 1, FR/HA 2, FR/LDR 4. **Total 15 units + 4 leaders.**
  - Page vs map: Page SP/LC 2; the map shows SP/LC 3 (two on the top row, one on the second). Map followed (SP 16 units instead of 15). A forum post (topic #6805, user Tarheel) says the booklet shows a Spanish LC in the back row that an older map lacked, which fits the map.

- **Guerrilla Action:** Rule in effect; Spanish start with **1** counter.
- **Objectives:** None beyond kill banners; Spanish hand shrinks as banners are lost.
- **Special rules (paraphrased):**
  - The Spanish hand is reduced by one Command card for each banner the Spanish lose; the French player picks the discarded card at random each time. Once the hand reaches three it goes no lower.

  - Spanish Guerrilla Action in effect, one starting counter.
  - The Guadiana and Hortiga Rivers are impassable except at the bridge.
- **Layout (indicative):** Guadiana River at the bottom left, Hortiga River at the right with a bridge near the bottom right. Spanish (top) in a long line along the top rows; French (bottom) in a left group, a right group and a small centre group. Mengabril village at the top-right corner.
- **New needs:**
  - Hand-size reduction tied to banners lost, random card chosen by the opponent (OPEN).
  - Cap of two units in square (OPEN: at once or in total).
  - Spanish move first.

### 2.6 Open rulings questions

These are text ambiguities. They are not decided here.

1. **Guerrilla, who holds counters in mixed armies (115 and 109, 118).** In 115 the page says the British player starts with no counters, while the rule names the Spanish commander. Who may spend counters, and does the rule cover a French card played against a British/Portuguese-only part of the army?
2. **Guerrilla and card scope.** Counters nullify a French Command card on a French turn; the text excludes a French First Strike played on the Allied turn. Does the rule also cover Tactic cards that are not Command cards? The French draw a replacement card that ends the turn; confirm the replacement is not played.
3. **Guerrilla pool.** Six tokens exist. May the Spanish hold more than the starting counters (acquired by Scout cards), and is there a cap at six? Does the French die roll use plain saber hits only, or can a Tactician bonus change it?
4. **106 hand reduction.** The French pick a random card at each banner loss. If the loss comes mid-turn after a card has been played, is the card taken from the current hand at once, and does the floor of three apply to the number of cards held or to the hand size for the next draw?
5. **106 square limit.** "A maximum of two units" in square during the battle: at one time, or in total over the battle?
6. **102 exit banners.** Banners start only after three ordered Spanish units have exited. Must the exit be made as an ordered move, or does a retreat off the Spanish edge count too?
7. **101 Swiss.** The mass removal of the remaining French Swiss gives the Spanish nothing; does it count as an elimination for any other rule?
8. **114 and 117 banner timing.** Settled by base ruling R66 (design/rulings.md on main); restate it for the Spanish battles and check that it covers a majority that changes during the opponent's turn.
9. **115 Stewart's Folly.** The text names the British player; do Spanish and Portuguese units also lose the ability to form square?
10. **109 Portina Brook.** Settled by base ruling R47 (design/rulings.md on main); check that the brook rule is the same as the base brook and that nothing else is needed.
